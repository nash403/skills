What you can do on this page

Right-click a criterion to cross it out, hide it, write your own note, or filter to the criteria around its source line.

Click a source to copy its full repo-relative path, or a 📝 marker to copy that note. Hover either to read it in full.

Tick criteria to copy them as a Markdown table, your own notes included. Shift-click ticks a whole range, and the Actions menu marks or annotates the whole selection at once.

Filter by flag with the coloured buttons, and jump between zones from the index above or by clicking the zone title while you scroll.

Search reads the ID, the criterion, and both kinds of note. A file path matches only as an exact prefix or suffix, and File.tsx:112~10 matches criteria within ten lines of 112.

The workspace, in the corner, holds notes about the report as a whole rather than about one criterion.

Everything you mark lives in this browser alone, and survives a reload.

Share your work from the configurations button: copy yours, or import one you were sent. An import sits alongside yours, it never replaces it.
