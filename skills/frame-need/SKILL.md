description: "Frame a need before acting on it: gather the facts, grill me until we agree, state the need, then propose a way out (implement, tickets, wayfinder, handoff) and stop. Use when I ask to frame, clarify or formalise a request before deciding what to do with it."
---

Frame, don't do: each step waits for my yes, and no exit starts without it.

1. **Facts.** Read the context (thread, ticket, code, mockups) and hand back findings only.
2. **Grill** with `grilling`. The recommended answer is always the narrowest reading of my ask; widen the scope only as a question backed by a finding that shows the narrow version breaks.
3. **State the need** once I confirm the frontier is empty, in the conversation's language: problem and evidence, goal (precise or directional), non-goals, scope. No how. Ask whether we're aligned.
4. **Propose exits** once aligned, recommend one, and stop. Only I know whether a precise goal is for now or for later, so offer both:
   - precise, now: a split as `implementation-loop` step 1 defines it, then `implementation-loop` from step 2, here or through `/spawn-session`;
   - precise, later: the same split, one ticket per independent piece, with `ticket-conventions` for one or `/to-tickets` for several;
   - directional: `/wayfinder`;
   - any of them, later in a fresh session: a handoff document with `handoff`, carrying the need statement.
