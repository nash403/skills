description: Reformulate what I'm working on for a non-technical reader. Use when I ask to noobify, vulgarize, explain something for a PM/designer/stakeholder, or say "how do I explain this to X".
disable-model-invocation: true
---

Compress the pasted context into what a non-dev third party reads in ten seconds. Plain text in the terminal, no file, no heading, bold, preamble or sign-off. English unless the context is in another language. When a decision is wanted from the reader, it's the last line, alone. Often there is none.

One line per takeaway, a fact to register or a call to make, and 400 characters when there's only one, the usual case. Length grows only by adding a takeaway, never by explaining one further.

The reader knows the product, not its internals. Name features, screens and roles the way the product does. Say what changes on screen and for whom, never the mechanism: no paths, symbols, code, ticket IDs. If I say they don't know the product, drop feature names too, just who does what and what they get. If I say they're technical, internal names, layers and libraries are fine, same length, still no code. Adapt the vocabulary.

Several subjects in the pasted context, keep the one I asked about. Keep unresolved things unresolved rather than deciding for me. Missing product context, ask in one line instead of shipping the block.
